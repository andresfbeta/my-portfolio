# Surface brief — WiddyUp case study

<!-- impeccable: confirmed from plan + critique 2026-08-06; interview skipped (user approved full redesign plan) -->

## Mode
Experience (portfolio case study) with staffing conversion at the close.

## Job and audience
Agency/studio design lead deciding whether to staff Andrés. Must understand the AI-accelerated validation loop and leave ready to contact.

## Outcome and proof
Primary action at end: Get in touch. Proof: real WiddyUp screens, process decisions (IA cut order, mid-fi iterations), AI tool chain (Claude → Figma Make → Figma). No invented metrics/quotes.

## Selected direction
- Visual authority: incumbent WiddyUp brand (Indigo/Teal, Sora + DM Sans) — preserve tokens; redesign composition.
- Thesis: product artifact leads the first viewport; AI process appears right after problem context; component library is a thin financial demo, not a zoo.
- Sequence: Hero (brand + phones + meta) → Context → Tools/AI → Architecture → Flows → Iteration → System → Result → Close (contact primary).

## Scope and boundaries
- Touch: `proyectos/widdyup.html`, `css/case-widdyup.css`, `js/i18n-widdyup.js`, `js/lightbox.js`
- Untouched: home, AllCore360, factual claims, brand palette/fonts
- Anti-goals: new color world; more decorative cards; “See more work” as primary CTA

## Interaction and layout
- Mobile: phones in first screen; meta after phones
- Kickers removed (headings carry weight)
- Lightbox: focus trap + i18n; lazy mid-page images; AA contrast; 44px CTAs
